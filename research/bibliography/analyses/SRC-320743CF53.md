# Επιστημονική ανάλυση — SRC-320743CF53

Seminal JMLR survey of transfer learning in RL. It defines evaluation dimensions including jumpstart, asymptotic performance, total reward/AUC, transfer ratio and time-to-threshold, and distinguishes total source+target cost from target-only adaptation cost.

These ideas are scientifically valuable but already represented in the current thesis protocol by selected `SRC-67AB8572A9` and `SRC-19858252B7`, which are closer to modern continual/safe-transfer questions and explicitly support negative-transfer/no-transfer controls.

**Decision:** reject as historically important but redundant transfer survey. Its metrics do not need an additional independent citation in the core corpus.
