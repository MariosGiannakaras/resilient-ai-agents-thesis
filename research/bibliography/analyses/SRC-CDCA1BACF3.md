## Source
Dimitris Konstantakopoulos, **Vulnerabilities and Robustness in Computer Vision**, University of Piraeus MSc thesis, 2024.

## Assessment
The work evaluates computer-vision models under dataset shift and adversarial attacks. It concerns supervised perception-model robustness, including ObjectNet and black-/white-box attacks, rather than sequential decision-making under changing transition/reward dynamics.

The thesis already distinguishes adversarial observation robustness from environmental resilience and includes stronger primary RL/OOD sources for the uncertainty mechanisms that matter experimentally.

## Decision
