# Scientific analysis — SRC-CCFF246CA7

## Source
Jakob Gawlikowski et al., **A survey of uncertainty in deep neural networks**, Artificial Intelligence Review, 2023.

## Assessment
This is a strong and comprehensive survey of uncertainty sources, epistemic/model uncertainty, aleatoric/data uncertainty, Bayesian neural networks, ensembles, calibration, and uncertainty evaluation. It is scientifically relevant at the general deep-learning level.

For the thesis, however, it does not add a unique claim beyond already selected uncertainty sources that are closer to the actual experimental design: the uncertainty taxonomy, MC-dropout/background sources, RL-specific OOD detection work, and the newly selected Deep Ensembles paper. The thesis does not need another broad DNN-UQ survey to justify the same epistemic/aleatoric and calibration distinctions.

## Scope boundary
The survey is not an online non-stationary RL study and does not establish a sequential changepoint detector, recovery mechanism, or resource-aware adaptation policy.

## Decision
**Rejected from the curated thesis set because of redundancy, not quality.** The original source remains preserved.
