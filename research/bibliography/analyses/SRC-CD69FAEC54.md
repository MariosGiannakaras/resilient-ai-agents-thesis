# Επιστημονική ανάλυση — SRC-CD69FAEC54

High-quality theoretical overview of MARL in stochastic/extensive-form games. It explicitly discusses non-stationarity caused by other concurrently learning agents.

That causal mechanism is precisely outside the thesis core: the single learner faces exogenous environmental changes rather than policy-induced changes from co-learners. Direct MARL surveys already analyzed confirm this boundary.

**Decision:** reject due to MARL scope; it remains a useful contrast reference.
