# Scientific analysis — SRC-55005EF367

## Source
Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto, **Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction — Second edition, in progress**, repository copy dated 2014–2015.

## Assessment
The stored text is an early pre-publication draft of the second edition. It contains the foundational material needed for tabular RL, including incremental action-value methods, tracking non-stationary bandits, finite MDPs, dynamic programming, Monte Carlo methods, and Q-learning.

However, this record should not be cited as the 2018 final edition. The official MIT Press second edition was published in 2018 and is represented separately by `SRC-701E163AC8`. Publisher metadata confirms the final second edition and its tabular/non-stationary/Q-learning coverage.

## Decision
**Rejected as a superseded pre-publication draft.** It remains preserved for provenance; the final-edition record `SRC-701E163AC8` is the canonical Sutton–Barto target.
