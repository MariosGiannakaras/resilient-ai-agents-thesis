# Επιστημονική ανάλυση — SRC-732D12A2CC

Recent framework operationalizing reliability for modern AI agents through consistency, robustness, predictability and safety, with experiments on agentic/LLM benchmarks such as GAIA and τ-bench.

The reliability decomposition is interesting, especially trajectory/resource consistency and the distinction between capability and reliability. However, the experimental object is LLM/tool-using agents, not reinforcement-learning policies under controlled MDP changepoints. The thesis already has RL-specific recovery, safety, statistical and resource-accounting metrics.

**Decision:** reject from the core due to different agent paradigm; retain as external-validity background for broader AI-agent reliability discussion.
