# Scientific analysis — SRC-F6E2AD5FA8

## Source
Dionysios Bakis, **Machine Learning-based Surrogate Models for Uncertainty Quantification in CFD**, NTUA diploma thesis, 2024.

## Assessment
The thesis studies uncertainty quantification for aerodynamic design using CFD, Monte Carlo, polynomial chaos, and neural surrogate models. Its uncertainty is parametric/model uncertainty in computational fluid dynamics and robust engineering design, not uncertainty faced by an online reinforcement-learning agent after an environmental change.

It therefore does not contribute a detector, adaptation mechanism, recovery metric, or RL benchmark relevant to the thesis.

## Decision
**Rejected as domain- and problem-mismatched UQ material.** The bilingual original is preserved without translation.
