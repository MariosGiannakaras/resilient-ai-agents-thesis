# Επιστημονική ανάλυση — SRC-02655A05A2

Comprehensive peer-reviewed review categorizing robust RL into transition-, disturbance-, action- and observation-robust designs and linking them to risk/entropy formulations.

The taxonomy is scientifically useful, but all four perturbation classes and the robustness-versus-adaptation boundary are already covered by selected primary papers and the thesis's consolidated perturbation taxonomy. Adding another broad review would increase redundancy rather than change the protocol.

**Decision:** reject as high-quality but redundant robust-RL review.
