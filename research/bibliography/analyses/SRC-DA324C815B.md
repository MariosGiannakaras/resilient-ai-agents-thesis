# Επιστημονική ανάλυση — SRC-DA324C815B

Technically strong offline RL work using large-deviation uncertainty sets, distributionally robust off-policy evaluation and non-rectangular robust optimization from a single correlated trajectory.

The central setting prohibits further environment interaction and optimizes/evaluates policies from a fixed logged trajectory. That is fundamentally different from the thesis's online post-change adaptation problem. The non-rectangular robustness and support-shift issues are already covered by direct robust/offline boundary sources.

**Decision:** reject due to offline-RL problem setting and redundancy.
