# Scientific analysis — SRC-698B096865

## Source
Antonios Mavrotas, **Q-Res MARL: A Resilience-Based Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Framework for Post-Earthquake Recovery of Interdependent Infrastructures**, TU Delft, 2025.

## Assessment
The thesis optimizes post-earthquake infrastructure repair scheduling with multi-agent RL and community-resilience objectives. Its notion of resilience concerns restoration of interdependent physical infrastructure after a disaster, while its learning setup introduces multiple agents, centralized/decentralized coordination, and domain-specific recovery objectives.

The current thesis benchmark is a single-agent environment with exogenous changes. Importing this source as core evidence would conflate system/infrastructure recovery with learning-agent recovery and would add MARL coordination confounds.

## Decision
**Rejected because its resilience target and MARL setting are outside the experimental scope.** The source remains archived.
