# Επιστημονική ανάλυση — SRC-958647DE4E

Seminal Madry et al. work framing adversarial robustness of deep classifiers as robust optimization against bounded malicious perturbations.

It is a foundational adversarial-ML source, but it is supervised classification rather than reinforcement learning and assumes an explicit first-order adversary. The thesis core is non-adversarial environmental uncertainty; adversarial observation robustness is already explicitly separated from ordinary noise.

**Decision:** reject due to supervised/adversarial threat model despite foundational status.
