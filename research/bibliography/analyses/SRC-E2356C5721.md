# Scientific analysis — SRC-E2356C5721

## Source
Jordi Smit, **Know what it does not know: Improving Offline Deep Reinforcement Learning with Uncertainty Estimation**, TU Delft MSc thesis, 2021.

## Assessment
The work studies epistemic uncertainty and pessimism in **offline** deep RL, where a policy is learned from a fixed dataset and must avoid extrapolation to poorly covered state-action pairs. This is a scientifically coherent use of uncertainty, but the causal problem is dataset coverage and offline value overestimation rather than an online environmental changepoint.

The thesis being developed here studies agents interacting online with changing environments and measures detection, adaptation, and recovery. Offline pessimism does not directly provide those mechanisms. General uncertainty concepts are already covered by stronger and more directly relevant selected sources.

## Decision
**Rejected because offline dataset uncertainty is not the same problem as online environmental uncertainty and recovery.** Original material is retained.
