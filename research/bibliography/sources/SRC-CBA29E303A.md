# Revisiting Fundamentals of Experience Replay

William Fedus, Prajit Ramachandran, Rishabh Agarwal, Yoshua Bengio, Hugo Larochelle, Mark Rowland, Will Dabney

Proceedings of the 37th International Conference on Machine Learning, PMLR 119:3061-3071, 2020.

Canonical article page: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/fedus20a.html

Protocol-v2 closure relevance: studies replay capacity and replay ratio as consequential properties of off-policy deep RL. This source supports freezing and reporting DQN replay capacity/update ratio and treating replay state/management as part of the scientific configuration and continuation state rather than an invisible library default. It does not justify post-change replay reset or recency weighting unless those are separate predeclared interventions.
