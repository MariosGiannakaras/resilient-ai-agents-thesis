Lectures from ECE524 Foundations of Reinforcement Learning at Princeton University, Spring 2024. This course is a graduate level course, focusing on theoretical foundations of reinforcement learning. It covers basics of Markov Decision Process (MDP), dynamic programming-based algorithms, planning, exploration, information theoretical lower bounds as well as how to leverage offline data. Various advanced topics are also discussed, including policy optimization, function approximation, multiagency and partial observability. This course puts special emphases on the algorithms and their theoretical analyses. Prior knowledge on linear algebra, probability and statistics is required.
