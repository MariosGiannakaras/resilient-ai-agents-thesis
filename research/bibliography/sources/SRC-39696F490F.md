## Intake rationale

Canonical broad review for continual/lifelong/non-stationary reinforcement learning. It is needed to place the thesis's five deliberately simple tabular strategies inside the wider literature without implying that GridWorld defines the research topic.

## Theory topics to extract during scientific analysis

- Formal scope and drivers of non-stationarity.
- Continual RL as incremental decision making under changing tasks/environment components.
- Stability–plasticity and adaptation/retention tensions.
- Taxonomy of continual RL approaches and the role of model-free, model-based, context, memory, detection and exploration mechanisms.
- Evaluation dimensions and why adaptation after change needs more than final return alone.
- Limits of transferring stationary convergence assumptions into non-stationary settings.
