# Empirical Design in Reinforcement Learning

Andrew Patterson, Samuel Neumann, Martha White, Adam White

Journal of Machine Learning Research, 25(318):1-63, 2024.

Canonical article page: https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v25/23-0183.html

This methodological paper develops guidance for empirical reinforcement-learning research, including performance distributions, independent runs, paired comparisons, confidence intervals, multiple-agent comparisons, environment selection, hyperparameter sensitivity, and experimenter bias. It is required for the protocol-v2 fairness contract, especially the use of agent-environment interactions rather than episode count as the primary learning budget and the separation of tuning from statistical replication.
