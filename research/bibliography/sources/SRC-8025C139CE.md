## Intake rationale

Focused survey of single-agent reinforcement learning when the stationary-environment assumption is relaxed. It complements the broader continual-RL literature by concentrating directly on algorithms intended to react/adapt to varying operating conditions.

## Theory topics to extract during scientific analysis

- Why stationary-MDP assumptions are restrictive for dynamic deployments.
- Categories of methods for changing reward/dynamics/task conditions.
- Adaptation versus robustness versus change detection/restart/context mechanisms.
- Regret/performance-loss perspectives under non-stationarity.
- Appropriate interpretation of simple continual value updating as a baseline rather than a general solution to dynamic environments.
- Experimental-design lessons for comparing adaptation mechanisms under controlled changes.
