Summary
With the ever increasing complexity of the upcoming mobile cellular networks, due to the proliferation of connected heterogeneous end devices, and the constant increase in computing performance, the existence of an Artificial Intelligence (AI) - Driven framework for network system automation is of high importance. However, as progressing towards the Network Intelligentization era, which enables on-device intelligence, a necessity can already be identified for a novel AI-enabled networking paradigm that will identify the diverse and heterogeneous network entities along with their respective computing capabilities towards supporting such complex AI-enabled network system. Initially, this thesis provides a presentation of the state-of-the-art AI solutions for computation-oriented communications, focusing on the energy aspect. From the state-of-the-art analysis, it is easily observed that a well-known AI framework, namely the Federated Learning (FL) is an emerging technique. Next, an extensive description of the network system is presented, while providing a comprehensive system model. An extensive description of the problem formulation towards a AI-driven networking system is also provided. To this end, a Soft Actor Critic (SAC) Reinforcement Learning (RL) solution is proposed targeting the minimization of the overall energy consumption of a FL process. The effectiveness of the proposed scheme is presented with results compared to three state-of-the-art baseline solutions.
