# The Primacy Bias in Deep Reinforcement Learning

Evgenii Nikishin, Max Schwarzer, Pierluca D'Oro, Pierre-Luc Bacon, Aaron Courville

Proceedings of the 39th International Conference on Machine Learning, PMLR 162:16828-16847, 2022.

Canonical article page: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/nikishin22a.html

Protocol-v2 closure relevance: documents primacy bias in deep RL, where early experience can exert a persistent influence and later evidence may be underused. This source is relevant to interpreting ordinary continued DQN/PPO learning after environmental change and to the threat-of-validity discussion. The paper's reset intervention is not adopted by default: any reset/plasticity mitigation would be a distinct predeclared scientific intervention rather than a transparent continuation detail.
