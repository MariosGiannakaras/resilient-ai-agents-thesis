## Intake rationale

Primary Dyna source directly describing learned world models, planning with simulated experience, Dyna-Q, and changing-world experiments. It is especially important because it experimentally separates ordinary Dyna-Q from an exploration-bonus variant that became known as Dyna-Q+, matching the thesis's mechanism-level comparison.

## Theory topics to extract during scientific analysis

- Integration of real experience, learned forward model, and incremental planning.
- Dyna-Q as Q-learning augmented with model learning and planning backups.
- Planning budget as a computational/algorithmic dimension rather than extra environment interaction.
- Changing-world experiments and why stale learned models create a need for continued exploration.
- Exploration-bonus/recency mechanism, handling of previously untried actions, and the conceptual distinction between plain Dyna-Q and Dyna-Q+.
