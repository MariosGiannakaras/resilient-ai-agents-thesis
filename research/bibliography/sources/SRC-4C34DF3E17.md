# Loss of plasticity in deep continual learning

Shibhansh Dohare, J. Fernando Hernandez-Garcia, Qingfeng Lan, Parash Rahman, A. Rupam Mahmood, Richard S. Sutton

Nature 632, 768-774 (2024).

DOI: 10.1038/s41586-024-07711-7

This peer-reviewed study demonstrates loss of plasticity in standard deep-learning systems under continual learning, including a non-stationary reinforcement-learning experiment using PPO with abrupt changes in environment friction. It distinguishes loss of plasticity from catastrophic forgetting and provides direct evidence that ordinary PPO continued training can become progressively less able to adapt. Protocol-v2 therefore treats its Continual deep-agent branch as ordinary continued training, not as a specialized continual-learning algorithm; plasticity-preserving interventions remain outside the default final matrix unless separately justified.
