It provides the full-stack foundation and extensive developer choice you need to transform your applications and workflows into powerful, reliable agentic systems at global scale.

- Features

### Design agents and multi-agent workflows

- Create sophisticated multi-agent systems with simplicity using [Agent Development Kit](https://google.github.io/adk-docs) (ADK). Build production-ready agents in under 100 lines of intuitive Python Code, Java and with support for more languages coming soon. ADK gives you precise control over how agents think, reason, and collaborate through deterministic guardrails and orchestration controls. You can interact with your agents in human-like conversations with ADK's unique bidirectional audio and video streaming capabilities.

- Jumpstart your development with [Agent Garden](https://console.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/agents/agent-garden): a collection of ready-to-use samples and tools directly accessible within ADK.

- With ADK, you can choose your preferred model or deployment target. Not tied to our tools? No problem. You can also build agents built with popular open-source frameworks like [LangChain](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/agent-engine/develop/langchain), [LangGraph](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/agent-engine/develop/langgraph), [AG2](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/agent-engine/develop/ag2) or Crew.ai, or other alternatives that fit your preferences.

### Make diverse agents communicate and work together

- Connect any agent, anywhere with the open [Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol](https://google.github.io/A2A). This universal communication standard enables agents across different ecosystems to communicate with each other, irrespective of the framework (ADK, LangGraph, Crew.ai, or others) or vendor they are built on. Using A2A, agents can publish their capabilities and negotiate how they will interact with users (through text, forms, or bidirectional audio/video)–all while working securely together.

- Like APIs but for agent communication, A2A lets you turn isolated agents into collaborative teams, freeing you from concerns about investing in incompatible agent frameworks. A2A is a collaborative effort to standardize agent interoperability with contributions from a growing ecosystem of [50+ partners](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/partners/best-agentic-ecosystem-helping-partners-build-ai-agents-next25) including Box, Deloitte, Elastic, Salesforce, ServiceNow, UiPath, UKG, and many more.

### Connect your agents with the right tools, data, and guardrails

- You can equip agents with your existing enterprise truth data without building from scratch, using any approach you prefer:

- ADK supportsModel Context Protocol (MCP), so your agents connect to the vast and diverse data sources or capabilities you already rely on by leveraging the growing ecosystem of MCP-compatible tools.

- You can also connect your agents to enterprise systems through 100+ pre-built [connectors](https://cloud.google.com/integration-connectors/docs/overview), your custom APIs in [Apigee](https://cloud.google.com/apigee/docs), or workflows in [Application Integration](https://cloud.google.com/application-integration). These easy-to-use building blocks ensure your agents behave in alignment with your business rules and operate within appropriate guardrails.

- Using ADK, your agents can also tap into your existing API investments managed in [Apigee API Management](https://cloud.google.com/apigee)–irrespective of where they reside.

### Deploy agents to production with confidence

- Simplify your path to production with [Agent Engine](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/agent-engine/overview), which is a set of services that enables developers to deploy, manage, and scale AI agents in production. Agent Engine handles infrastructure management, scaling, security, and monitoring so you can focus on agent capabilities rather than operational concerns.

- You can deploy agents seamlessly regardless of which framework or model provider you've chosen. With Agent Engine, you can also keep the context in your conversations so your interactions with agents feel human-like, rather than starting from a blank slate each time.

- Agent Engine supports short-term memory and long-term memory. This way, you can manage your sessions and your agents can recall your past conversations and preferences.

- Lastly, with comprehensive evaluation tools and [Example Store](https://docs.cloud.google.com/agent-builder/agent-engine/example-store/overview) from Vertex AI you can improve agent performance and refine based on real-world usage.

### Enable your agents to understand and reason with your data

- Equip your agents with comprehensive retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) capabilities that let them intelligently access and interpret your organization's knowledge. [Vertex AI Search](https://cloud.google.com/enterprise-search) provides an out-of-the-box RAG solution that gets you started with just a few clicks, while [Vector Search](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/docs/vector-search/overview) supports hybrid techniques that combine vector-based and keyword-based approaches for more relevant responses. You can also build your own custom [RAG engine](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/rag-overview) by connecting to diverse data sources including local files, Cloud Storage, Google Drive, Slack, Jira, and many more.

### Ground AI responses in reliable data sources

- You can ground your AI responses with authoritative information like [Google Search](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/multimodal/ground-gemini) (powering 99% of the world's search data) or specialized data from providers like Cotality, Dun & Bradstreet, HGInsights, S&P Global, and Zoominfo.

- For agents that rely on geospatial context, you can also ground your agents with Google Maps. Currently available as an experimental capability starting with US customers, your AI can access [Google Maps data](https://mapsplatform.google.com/) that receives +100M updates daily* and covers +250M businesses and places globally*.

- **Grounding with Google Maps is currently available for testing in the United States as an experimental release, providing customers access to only places data and updates in the United States*

### Scale agent adoption across your enterprise

- Expand the reach of your custom AI agents by publishing them to Google [Agentspace](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/google-agentspace-enables-the-agent-driven-enterprise). This enterprise agent marketplace enables controlled sharing while maintaining centralized governance and monitoring. [Agentspace](https://cloud.google.com/products/agentspace) provides a single access point for all your organization's agents, making it easy for employees to discover and use the right AI tools for their needs. With just a few clicks, you can make your carefully crafted agents available to teams throughout your company, driving consistent AI experiences and maximizing your return on AI investments.

### Enterprise grade security for your AI agents

- Control agent output using [Gemini's built-in safety features](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/safety-guidance) like configurable content filters and system instructions that define boundaries around prohibited topics.

- Manage agent permissions through identity controls that let you determine whether agents operate with dedicated service accounts or on behalf of individual users. Protect sensitive data by confining agent activity within [secure perimeters](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/docs/general/vpc-service-controls). Establish guardrails around your agents to control interactions at every step–from screening inputs before they reach models to validating parameters before tool execution.

- Auto-monitor agent behavior using [comprehensive tracing capabilities](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/models/evaluation-overview) that give you visibility into every action an agent takes, including its reasoning process, tool selection, and execution paths.
