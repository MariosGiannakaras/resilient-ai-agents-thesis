## Intake rationale

Foundational primary source for tabular Q-learning. It gives the incremental action-value learning rule and the classical discrete-state/action convergence result under repeated sampling and the paper's stated assumptions. It is required to separate the formal stationary Q-learning result from the thesis's empirical question of adaptation after environmental change.

## Theory topics to extract during scientific analysis

- Q-learning as off-policy action-value control.
- Bellman-optimality target using the maximum next-state action value.
- Conditions attached to the classical convergence theorem.
- Difference between convergence in the stated Markovian/stationary setting and tracking a changing environment.
- Appropriate use as the common theoretical basis for Fixed Q-Learning and Adaptive Q-Learning, with the thesis-specific distinction being whether post-change updates are disabled or continued.
