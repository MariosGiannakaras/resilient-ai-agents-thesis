# Evidence — Non-stationary Reinforcement Learning without Prior Knowledge: An Optimal Black-box Approach

## E1 — Knowing the number or total amount of change is an oracle advantage

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Abstract; Section 1
- **Claim:** Many non-stationary learning algorithms require prior knowledge of how many changes will occur or how much the environment will vary, while MASTER is designed to adapt without receiving those quantities as inputs.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

Wei and Luo identify prior knowledge of the degree of non-stationarity as a central limitation of earlier methods. They use `L` for the number of changes and `Delta` for the amount of change and design a black-box reduction whose dynamic-regret guarantees adapt to these quantities without requiring the learner to know them in advance.

### Context and limits

A hyperparameter chosen from held-out development data is not the same as knowing the future test changepoints, but any use of true `L`, `Delta`, or change times should be labeled oracle information.

### Thesis use

Separate deployable non-oracle agents from periodic-reset or variation-budget baselines that use privileged knowledge.

### Citation

Wei and Luo (2021), Abstract and Section 1.

## E2 — Fixed restart periods, windows, and discount factors encode a change timescale

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Section 1 and Table 1 discussion
- **Claim:** A fixed forgetting schedule can become suboptimal when its timescale does not match the unknown rate of environmental change.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The paper contrasts MASTER with approaches based on restarting at a predetermined period, retaining a fixed-size window, or discounting old data with a fixed factor. In its regret analysis, these fixed-timescale strategies can have worse dependence on the actual number of changes when that scale is not known beforehand.

### Context and limits

This is a theoretical regret comparison. A fixed recency baseline can still be strong in a controlled environment whose shift frequency happens to match its tuning.

### Thesis use

For every forgetting baseline, record how the reset period, window length, or decay factor was selected and prohibit tuning on final test shifts.

### Citation

Wei and Luo (2021), Section 1 and Table 1 discussion.

## E3 — Multiple learner timescales can expose changes that a long-lived instance misses

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Section 2.3; Figure 1
- **Claim:** MASTER schedules base-algorithm instances with different durations so recently started instances remain sensitive to changes at different scales.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The reduction runs base learners over a hierarchy of randomized time intervals. Shorter-lived instances can adapt quickly to a newly changed environment, and their behavior can reveal that the performance expectations of a longer-running instance are no longer consistent with current observations. The design therefore covers several possible change scales without committing to one fixed window in advance.

### Context and limits

The full construction relies on confidence and regret properties of the base algorithm. A simple comparison of two moving averages is only an approximation to this idea and does not inherit MASTER's theorem.

### Thesis use

A lightweight short-versus-long evidence score may be explored as a pilot detector, but it must be evaluated as its own heuristic.

### Citation

Wei and Luo (2021), Section 2.3 and Figure 1.

## E4 — Detection and restart are separate stages in the adaptive mechanism

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Algorithm 3; Section 3.2
- **Claim:** MASTER applies stationarity tests and restarts its scheduling block when evidence indicates the current near-stationary assumptions have failed.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The algorithm maintains tests based on the performance and optimistic estimates of scheduled base instances. When a stationarity test is violated, the current block terminates and a new block begins. The evidence used to infer non-stationarity and the restart action taken afterward are therefore distinct parts of the procedure.

### Context and limits

The paper's thresholds are derived from assumptions about the base learner and cannot be transferred numerically to an unrelated Q-learning implementation.

### Thesis use

Log detector events and reset events separately, including false resets and any delay between a true change, detection, and policy intervention.

### Citation

Wei and Luo (2021), Algorithm 3 and Section 3.2.

## E5 — Adaptation to unknown `L` and `Delta` depends on base-algorithm guarantees

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Theorem 2 and Assumption 1
- **Claim:** MASTER's parameter-free dynamic-regret guarantee is conditional on the plugged-in stationary learner satisfying specified optimism and regret properties.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The black-box result does not turn an arbitrary learning rule into an optimally adaptive non-stationary algorithm. Its theorem assumes a base algorithm with a particular near-stationary performance guarantee and confidence structure; under those conditions, the wrapper adapts its regret to the realized number and amount of changes without knowing them as inputs.

### Context and limits

Vanilla tabular Q-learning does not automatically inherit this result merely by adding a reset trigger.

### Thesis use

Use MASTER as theoretical support for non-oracle multi-timescale adaptation, not as a theorem about the thesis's simpler baselines.

### Citation

Wei and Luo (2021), Assumption 1 and Theorem 2.

## E6 — Fully prior-free infinite-horizon adaptation still has extra cost and open cases

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Sections 4.2 and 5
- **Claim:** In communicating infinite-horizon MDPs, removing knowledge of both non-stationarity quantities and structural parameters such as the maximum diameter introduces additional overhead, and the fully optimal case remains unresolved in the paper.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The strongest infinite-horizon results require either information about the non-stationarity or the maximum MDP diameter. When none of these quantities is known, the paper combines MASTER with an additional bandit-over-RL layer and pays an extra regret term; eliminating this cost is left open.

### Context and limits

The phrase “without prior knowledge” in the title concerns the degree of non-stationarity under the stated assumptions, not the absence of every possible structural assumption or tuning parameter.

### Thesis use

Document every source of prior knowledge and structural information available to each agent rather than using a binary “parameter-free” label.

### Citation

Wei and Luo (2021), Sections 4.2 and 5.
