# Ανάλυση — Partial Models for Building Adaptive Model-Based Reinforcement Learning Agents

## Βιβλιογραφική ταυτότητα
- **Συγγραφείς:** Safa Alver, Ali Rahimi-Kalahroudi, Doina Precup
- **Έτος canonical έκδοσης:** 2025
- **Δημοσίευση:** Proceedings of the 3rd Conference on Lifelong Learning Agents, Proceedings of Machine Learning Research, vol. 274, pp. 959–977
- **Canonical landing page:** `https://proceedings.mlr.press/v274/alver25a.html`
- **Ελεγμένο πλήρες κείμενο:** author manuscript corresponding to arXiv:2405.16899, checked against the PMLR bibliographic record

## Σκοπός και ερευνητικό ερώτημα
Η εργασία εξετάζει γιατί σύγχρονοι deep model-based RL agents μπορούν να προσαρμόζονται ανεπαρκώς μετά από **τοπική** αλλαγή στο περιβάλλον. Το κύριο πρόβλημα εντοπίζεται στη διατήρηση ενός ακριβούς learned model όταν παλιά και νέα δεδομένα συνυπάρχουν σε monolithic replay buffers και monolithic learned models. Η προτεινόμενη λύση είναι η χρήση πολλών partial models, ώστε μόνο τα τμήματα του learned model που επηρεάζονται από την αλλαγή να ενημερώνονται επιλεκτικά.

## Μεθοδολογία
- Uses the Local Change Adaptation (LoCA) evaluation framework, in which a phase switch changes a local part of the task while evaluation spans the larger state space.
- The ordinary LoCA setting changes a local reward function. The paper also adds LoCA1 with stochastic rewards and LoCA2 with both stochastic rewards and a non-stationary transition distribution.
- The phase switch is not signaled directly to the agent; evaluation is performed periodically from broader initial-state distributions.
- Identifies three adaptation challenges for deep MBRL: interference versus forgetting in replay, proper/model-focused updating, and speed of adaptation.
- Proposes simple and scalable partial-model implementations. The scalable variant uses modular heads/indexing so the required local component can be refreshed without separately duplicating the whole model.
- Experiments instantiate partial models first in deep Dyna-Q on MountainCarLoCA and MiniGridLoCA, then in PlaNet and Dreamer on pixel-based ReacherLoCA and RandomReacherLoCA.
- Compares regular agents with partial-model and local-forgetting variants and evaluates both adaptivity and speed of Phase-2 adaptation.

## Κύρια ευρήματα με ακριβείς θέσεις
1. **Monolithic replay/model structures can impede local adaptation.** Abstract and Section 1 identify distribution-shift handling in a single replay buffer/model as a central reason deep MBRL may fail to maintain an accurate model after a local change.
2. **The paper distinguishes stale-data interference from forgetting of old-but-relevant experience.** Section 4 explains that a large replay buffer can preserve stale pre-change samples that interfere with the updated local model, while a small buffer can discard experience still needed for unchanged parts of the environment.
3. **The LoCA phase change is not directly announced.** Section 2 specifies that the agent receives no signal at the phase switch and must adapt while Phase-2 experience is locally restricted; periodic evaluation uses broader initial states.
4. **The authors extend the evaluation beyond deterministic reward change.** Section 3 introduces LoCA1 with stochastic rewards and LoCA2 with stochastic rewards plus a non-stationary transition component, making the adaptation test closer to environmental transition change than the original LoCA setup.
5. **Partial models improve adaptation in the studied agents and settings.** Sections 6–7 report that partial-model versions of deep Dyna-Q, PlaNet and Dreamer adapt to the local Phase-2 change, while regular counterparts can fail because their learned models remain inaccurate after the shift. The partial-model variants also adapt faster than the local-forgetting comparison in the reported setups.
6. **The result is intervention-specific rather than a general model-based superiority result.** Section 9 states that the experiments consider particular local-adaptation settings and additionally assume a useful initial exploration phase and a suitable state-similarity representation.

## Υποθέσεις και ορισμοί που πρέπει να διατηρηθούν
- LoCA studies a **local** environmental/task change and asks whether a model-based agent can update behavior elsewhere using local post-change evidence.
- Standard LoCA changes rewards; LoCA2 additionally changes a local transition distribution.
- The study's deep Dyna-Q is not the same implementation or scientific object as the thesis's bounded tabular Dyna-Q+ method.
- The paper evaluates specific partial-model interventions; plain model-based RL does not inherit their benefits automatically.
- The agent is assumed to have enough initial exploration to form useful state clusters/representations and a workable similarity signal.

## Περιορισμοί / threats to validity
- The environmental changes are intentionally local and structured. The thesis includes persistent action remapping, action failure and observation corruption under a separately frozen protocol, so LoCA evidence cannot be treated as direct equivalence.
- Deep Dyna-Q, PlaNet and Dreamer use learned neural models/replay machinery that differ materially from the thesis's tabular Dyna-Q+ implementation.
- The benefit comes from a particular modular partial-model design plus change/component identification; it is not evidence that model-based agents are generically more resilient than model-free agents.
- Section 9 acknowledges assumptions about initial exploration and the ability to measure useful state similarity.
- The LoCA criterion of near-optimal Phase-2 adaptation differs from the thesis's RQ2 difference-in-differences estimand and RQ3 fixed-window right-censored recovery contract.

## Σχέση με την υπάρχουσα βιβλιογραφία
Η πηγή προσθέτει πρόσφατη peer-reviewed evidence για model-based adaptation failure modes, stale replay/model information και modular local updating. Συμπληρώνει τις ήδη επιλεγμένες πηγές για continual RL, Dyna/model-based planning και non-stationarity, χωρίς να αντικαθιστά το foundational Dyna-Q/Dyna-Q+ evidence ή το frozen project-specific protocol. Είναι ιδιαίτερα χρήσιμη για να εξηγηθεί στη Discussion γιατί η κατοχή μοντέλου του περιβάλλοντος δεν αρκεί από μόνη της για resilience μετά από αλλαγή.

## Απόφαση
**Επιλογή ως υποστηρικτική πηγή.** Η peer-reviewed εργασία καλύπτει ένα σύγχρονο και άμεσα σχετικό κενό του final manuscript: πώς stale/monolithic learned models και replay μπορούν να περιορίσουν adaptation και πώς modular updates μπορούν να βοηθήσουν. Δεν αλλάζει κανένα frozen protocol, estimand ή scientific conclusion.
