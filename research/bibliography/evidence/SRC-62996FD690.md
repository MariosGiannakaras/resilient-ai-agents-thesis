# Evidence — Uncertainty-Based Out-of-Distribution Detection in Deep Reinforcement Learning

## E1 — Epistemic uncertainty can act as an OOD signal

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Abstract; Section 1
- **Claim:** The paper uses epistemic uncertainty from a value-estimation network to distinguish states that are poorly represented by training data.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

Sedlmeier et al. motivate their detector through the relationship between epistemic uncertainty and lack of data. In their value-based RL setting, states from low-density or previously unseen regions are expected to produce higher epistemic uncertainty, so the uncertainty estimate can be used as a score for separating in-distribution and out-of-distribution observations.

### Context and limits

The method is evaluated with deep value networks. An uncertainty score is not automatically a calibrated probability of OOD membership and is not a sequential changepoint detector by itself.

### Thesis use

Use this source only to motivate an optional uncertainty-based detection signal whose calibration, false-alarm rate, and temporal detection behavior are evaluated separately.

### Citation

Sedlmeier et al. (2019), Abstract and Section 1.

## E2 — A controlled GridWorld can create a leakage-safe held-out OOD region

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Section 3, Experimental Setup
- **Claim:** The study creates OOD states by reversing start and goal regions after training and keeps evaluation trajectories out of the training data.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The agent is trained in a 12×4 gridworld where starts are sampled from one half and goals from the other. For OOD evaluation, the start and goal regions are mirrored so the agent encounters states that were not visited during training. Evaluation runs use frozen model snapshots and their observations are not fed back into learning.

### Context and limits

This is a static train/test distribution shift. It does not measure adaptation or recovery after an online environmental changepoint.

### Thesis use

Use held-out structural configurations for leakage-safe OOD diagnostics and keep them separate from the online adaptation protocol.

### Citation

Sedlmeier et al. (2019), Section 3.

## E3 — Bootstrap-based uncertainty was more stable than dropout-based uncertainty in the studied task

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Sections 4–5; Figure 1
- **Claim:** Bootstrap ensemble variants maintained a more reliable separation between training and mirror environments than the tested Monte-Carlo dropout variants.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

Across training snapshots, the BOOT and BOOTP architectures generally assign higher epistemic uncertainty to the mirror environment than to the training environment, while the Monte-Carlo dropout variants show reversals in the relative uncertainty of the two environments. Adding the randomized prior increases the separation in the reported experiment.

### Context and limits

The result is specific to the tested architecture, gridworld, and training process. It does not establish universal superiority of bootstrap ensembles over dropout or other uncertainty estimators.

### Thesis use

Select any uncertainty estimator through held-out calibration and sequential detection tests rather than theoretical popularity alone.

### Citation

Sedlmeier et al. (2019), Sections 4–5 and Figure 1.

## E4 — OOD detection and environmental change detection remain different problems

- **Type:** scope inference grounded in the paper
- **Location:** Sections 1–5
- **Claim:** The paper classifies frozen evaluation states as in- or out-of-distribution; it does not infer the time of a persistent MDP regime change or specify a recovery action.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The proposed uncertainty score is evaluated by comparing observations from a training environment with observations from a separate mirror environment. There is no unknown within-stream changepoint, detector-to-adapter interface, or policy-recovery protocol in the study.

### Thesis use

Do not equate high epistemic uncertainty or static OOD classification with successful changepoint detection or resilience.

### Citation

Sedlmeier et al. (2019), overall method scope, Sections 1–5.
