# Evidence — Emergent Complexity and Zero-shot Transfer via Unsupervised Environment Design

## E1 — UED generates a training distribution from an underspecified environment
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Abstract; Section 1; Section 3
- **Claim:** Unsupervised Environment Design starts from a domain with free environment parameters and defines a procedure that produces a distribution over fully specified environments for continued policy training.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
Dennis et al. define UED as the problem of taking an underspecified environment, whose free parameters control features or behavior, and using an environment-design policy to generate fully specified environments that support the continued learning of the current agent policy. The developer specifies the domain and its degrees of freedom without having to enumerate the final training distribution manually.

### Context and limits
The choice of free parameters still constrains what UED can generate. UED cannot discover a type of environment variation that is absent from the underspecified domain representation.

### Thesis use
If procedural scenario generation is used, record the free parameters and generation rule explicitly so the resulting training distribution is reproducible.

### Citation
Dennis et al. (2020), Abstract and Sections 1 and 3.

## E2 — Domain randomization and pure minimax generation have different failure modes
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Abstract; Section 1; Figure 1
- **Claim:** Uniform random generation may fail to produce structured challenging environments, while a minimax adversary can be rewarded for constructing environments that are simply unsolvable.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
The paper argues that domain randomization samples environment parameters without adapting difficulty to the learner and can therefore spend much of its budget on unstructured or uninformative configurations. At the opposite extreme, an adversary trained only to minimize the protagonist's return can obtain high reward by making the task impossible rather than diagnostically difficult.

### Thesis use
Apply solvability checks and distinguish random/domain-randomized generation from adversarial stress generation in the benchmark protocol.

### Citation
Dennis et al. (2020), Abstract, Section 1, and Figure 1.

## E3 — PAIRED uses regret to favor difficult but achievable environments
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Sections 1 and 4
- **Claim:** PAIRED rewards an environment generator according to the performance gap between a protagonist and an antagonist, which removes the incentive to create tasks that neither agent can solve.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
PAIRED introduces an antagonist allied with the environment adversary. The adversary benefits when the antagonist succeeds where the protagonist performs poorly. If an environment is impossible for both agents, their returns are similar and the adversary obtains little or no regret reward. As the protagonist improves, the mechanism can shift toward environments that expose new performance gaps, producing an adaptive curriculum.

### Context and limits
The antagonist is a training device and creates an additional learner, optimization process, and source of stochasticity. Its presence changes the compute and tuning budget relative to simple scenario generation.

### Thesis use
Treat PAIRED as a feasibility/background method unless ordinary controlled scenario generation fails to cover the required stress cases.

### Citation
Dennis et al. (2020), Sections 1 and 4.

## E4 — Environment complexity should be measured, not assumed
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Section 5.1; Figure 2
- **Claim:** The navigation experiments quantify properties of generated environments such as obstacle count and path structure while tracking performance across multiple seeds.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
In the navigation domain, the authors monitor structural quantities including the number of blocks, goal distance, passable path length, and the path length achieved by agents. These measurements show how the generated curriculum changes over training rather than inferring difficulty solely from the generator's objective.

### Thesis use
For generated GridWorld layouts, log structural severity variables and a solvability diagnostic alongside agent return.

### Citation
Dennis et al. (2020), Section 5.1 and Figure 2.

## E5 — The reported transfer result is frozen-policy zero-shot generalization
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Sections 5–6
- **Claim:** After training, PAIRED policies are evaluated on previously unseen transfer environments without online policy updates.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
The experiments test trained policies on challenging hand-designed navigation environments and on unseen parameter settings in continuous control. The purpose is to measure zero-shot transfer from the training curriculum to novel test conditions. The policy is not presented as detecting a changepoint and then relearning during the test environment.

### Context and limits
Zero-shot transfer measures generalization of a frozen policy. It must remain separate from recovery time, post-shift adaptation rate, or repeated-change learning.

### Thesis use
Run frozen zero-shot evaluation before enabling any online adaptation and report the two phases separately.

### Citation
Dennis et al. (2020), Sections 5–6.

## E6 — UED complexity is not necessary when relevant cases can be specified directly
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Discussion and conclusion
- **Claim:** Automated environment design is most valuable when the relevant environment distribution or edge cases are difficult to enumerate manually.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
The motivation for UED is that realistic domains may contain too many important configurations for a designer to enumerate or weight correctly. When a small controlled study can explicitly specify the relevant perturbations and test cases, the additional adversarial curriculum machinery is not automatically justified.

### Thesis use
Prefer the simplest reproducible scenario generator that covers the thesis hypotheses; promote UED/PAIRED only if the controlled generator is demonstrably insufficient.

### Citation
Dennis et al. (2020), discussion and conclusion.
