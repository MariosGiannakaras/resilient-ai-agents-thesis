# Evidence — Safe Reinforcement Learning via Recovery-based Shielding with Gaussian Process Dynamics Models

## E1 — The safety architecture combines an online learned dynamics model with a trusted backup policy
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Abstract; Introduction; Contributions
- **Claim:** The proposed shield predicts whether a task-policy action may violate safety constraints and transfers control to a precomputed safe backup policy when necessary.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
Goodall and Belardinelli combine a Gaussian-process dynamics model with recovery-based shielding. The task agent proposes actions, while the shield uses the learned dynamics model and its uncertainty estimates to evaluate safety. When the proposed action cannot be certified, a trusted backup controller takes over to keep the state on a recoverable safe trajectory.

### Thesis use
Report the backup-controller prior, intervention count/rate, backup dwell time, and the difference between raw task proposals and executed actions.

### Citation
Goodall and Belardinelli (2026), Abstract, Introduction, and Contributions.

## E2 — The safety guarantee depends on calibrated uncertainty and regularity assumptions
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Contributions; Sections 2–3
- **Claim:** The high-probability safety lower bound is conditional on the GP dynamics model and assumptions such as bounded disturbances, known observation-noise structure, and regularity of the unknown dynamics.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
The method learns unknown nonlinear dynamics with Gaussian processes and uses analytic uncertainty propagation to verify actions. Its guarantee is derived under stated assumptions about the system, disturbance support, observation noise, smoothness, the backup controller, and calibration of the learned GP model.

### Context and limits
A confidence interval is part of a model-dependent guarantee, not an unconditional certificate valid after arbitrary structural dynamics changes.

### Thesis use
Record all model assumptions and revalidation conditions for any learned safety shield.

### Citation
Goodall and Belardinelli (2026), Contributions and Sections 2–3.

- **Type:** thesis-protocol implication grounded in the guarantee

## E5 — Recovery-based shielding is not task-policy recovery after an environmental shift
- **Type:** scope synthesis grounded in the method
- **Location:** Overall formulation
- **Claim:** “Recovery” refers to steering the physical/control system back toward a recoverable safe trajectory; it is not a changepoint detector or a measure of relearning the task policy after non-stationarity.
- **Status:** verified

### Thesis use
Keep `safety_recovery` distinct from `post_shift_task_recovery` in terminology and metrics.

Goodall and Belardinelli (2026), overall formulation.
