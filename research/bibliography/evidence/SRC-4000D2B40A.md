# Evidence — Behaviour Suite for Reinforcement Learning

## E1 — A diagnostic experiment includes environment, interaction regime, and analysis

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Section 2
- **Claim:** bsuite defines each experiment through a fixed set of parameterized environments, a fixed agent–environment interaction regime, and a fixed analysis procedure.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

Osband et al. state that a bsuite experiment contains three components: the environments, the interaction protocol, and the analysis that maps observed behavior to results and plots. The benchmark therefore standardizes more than the simulator itself.

### Thesis use

Version every resilience scenario together with its interaction budget, update rules, and analysis procedure; do not compare agents under different post-processing or budgets.

### Citation

Osband et al. (2019), Section 2.

## E2 — Diagnostic tests should be targeted, simple, challenging, scalable, and fast

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Section 2, experiment-design criteria
- **Claim:** A good diagnostic isolates a specific capability, removes unnecessary confounds, stresses the agent, varies systematically in difficulty, and remains fast enough for iteration.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The paper lists five qualities for bsuite experiments: targeted, simple, challenging, scalable, and fast. The goal is to isolate one important RL capability and reveal how it scales, rather than to build one complicated environment whose many factors make failures hard to interpret.

### Thesis use

Use independent severity sweeps for observation noise, transition noise, reward changes, obstacles, and action failures before combining factors.

### Citation

Osband et al. (2019), Section 2.

## E3 — Standard comparison can remain behavior-based and architecture-independent

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Section 2, behavioral measurement discussion
- **Claim:** bsuite standard analysis measures only properties observable through the environment, enabling comparison across algorithms and codebases without requiring internal-state access.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The suite deliberately bases its standard metrics on externally observable agent behavior. Internal beliefs, neural activations, and implementation-specific diagnostics may still be studied, but they are not required for the common benchmark score.

### Thesis use

Base cross-agent comparison on trajectories, returns, success, safety violations, interventions, and recovery. Keep neural or agent-specific diagnostics secondary.

### Citation

Osband et al. (2019), Section 2.

## E4 — Parameter sweeps expose scaling limits hidden by a single task instance

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Section 2.1 and other parameterized experiment descriptions
- **Claim:** Varying a task parameter across a range reveals where a capability ceases to scale.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The memory-length experiment varies how many sequential steps an agent must retain information, and the suite uses similar parameterization for other capabilities. Performance over a sweep therefore provides a capability profile instead of a binary result on one fixed environment.

### Thesis use

Report severity–performance and severity–recovery curves for each perturbation under a fixed interaction budget.

### Citation

Osband et al. (2019), Section 2.1 and parameterized experiment descriptions.

## E5 — Aggregate scores are summaries, not substitutes for diagnostic curves

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Sections 1.2 and 2
- **Claim:** bsuite provides normalized scores for quick comparison but explicitly positions itself as a diagnostic suite rather than a leaderboard.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

Each experiment maps behavior to a score in `[0,1]` for at-a-glance comparison, while the accompanying analysis notebooks expose detailed plots and measurements. The authors explicitly state that bsuite is not intended to become a leaderboard to optimize at the expense of understanding agent behavior.

### Thesis use

If a composite resilience score is reported, always publish the underlying per-perturbation curves and uncertainty intervals.

### Citation

Osband et al. (2019), Sections 1.2 and 2.

## E6 — Diagnostic suites do not establish general intelligence or external validity

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Sections 1.2–1.3
- **Claim:** The authors present bsuite as an evolving collection of targeted experiments, not a comprehensive substitute for larger benchmarks or proof of broad capability.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The paper explicitly says the suite does not capture all or even most important RL issues and should complement rather than replace large integrated challenge environments. Its purpose is to make particular capabilities concrete, measurable, and reproducible.

### Thesis use

Frame the GridWorld benchmark as a controlled diagnostic study whose conclusions are scoped to the tested perturbations and agents.

### Citation

Osband et al. (2019), Sections 1.2–1.3.
