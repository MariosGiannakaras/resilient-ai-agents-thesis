# Evidence — Efficient Action Robust Reinforcement Learning with Probabilistic Policy Execution Uncertainty

## E1 — Action uncertainty separates intended from executed actions

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Abstract; Section 1
- **Claim:** The paper studies uncertainty in which the action selected by the policy may differ from the action actually executed.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

Liu et al. model probabilistic policy-execution uncertainty by executing the agent's intended policy with probability `1-rho` and allowing an alternative adversarial action with probability `rho`. This explicitly distinguishes the agent's decision from the action that reaches the environment.

### Context and limits

The adverse replacement is a worst-case modeling device. Physical actuator failures need not be adversarial in practice.

### Thesis use

Log `intended_action` and `executed_action` separately and define the action-failure process explicitly.

### Citation

Liu et al. (2024), Abstract and Section 1.

## E2 — The uncertainty radius is an explicit severity parameter

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Section 3, Equation 3
- **Claim:** `rho` controls the amount of probabilistic action-execution uncertainty around the intended policy.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The actual behavior policy belongs to an uncertainty set centered on the agent policy and is written as a convex mixture of the intended policy and an arbitrary adversarial policy. Increasing `rho` increases the maximum probability with which the alternative action mechanism controls execution.

### Context and limits

`rho` is a model parameter, not automatically a calibrated probability of real hardware failure.

### Thesis use

Use an explicit severity grid for action substitution and keep training-time robustness settings distinct from deployment-time test severity.

### Citation

Liu et al. (2024), Section 3, Equation 3.

## E3 — A deterministic optimal robust policy exists for the paper's finite tabular setting

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Section 4, Theorem 1 and Equations 5–6
- **Claim:** Under the stated finite episodic uncertainty model, the robust problem admits a deterministic optimal policy and a Bellman-style optimality recursion.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The robust Bellman equation combines the value of the best intended action with the value of the worst alternative action, weighted by `1-rho` and `rho`. The theorem establishes existence of a deterministic optimal robust policy for the finite tabular formulation.

### Context and limits

The result does not automatically extend to arbitrary neural function approximation or a different action-failure model.

### Thesis use

Use this as theoretical support for a tabular action-robust comparator if the implementation remains computationally feasible.

### Citation

Liu et al. (2024), Section 4, Theorem 1, Equations 5–6.

## E4 — Training uncertainty and test perturbation should remain separate experimental parameters

- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Experimental section
- **Claim:** The paper trains under a specified uncertainty model and evaluates performance under varied action-perturbation conditions.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The experiments distinguish the uncertainty setting used to construct the robust learner from the probability and type of action perturbation used at evaluation time. This makes robustness to matched and mismatched perturbation severity observable rather than hiding both under one parameter.

### Thesis use

Record the robustness budget used during training separately from the action-failure probability used at test time.

### Citation

Liu et al. (2024), experimental section.

## E5 — Robust action training targets worst-case degradation, not online recovery

- **Type:** scope inference grounded in the formulation
- **Location:** Sections 3–9
- **Claim:** ARRLC is trained to perform well under a predefined policy-execution uncertainty set; it does not detect an unknown environmental changepoint or relearn after one.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase

The method optimizes a robust policy before evaluation under action perturbations. The paper does not introduce a separate change detector, context memory, reset mechanism, or post-change adaptation trajectory. Its contribution is therefore action robustness rather than resilience through online recovery.

### Thesis use

Keep action-robust baselines in the static/worst-case robustness category and compare them with adaptive agents using separate recovery metrics.

### Citation

Liu et al. (2024), method and experiment scope, Sections 3–9.
