# Evidence — Action Robust Reinforcement Learning and Applications in Continuous Control

## Evidence E1 — Action-execution uncertainty can be modeled as probabilistic control loss
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Abstract; Sections 1 and 3
- **Claim:** In the PR-MDP criterion, the agent proposes an action but with probability α an alternative, potentially adversarial, action is executed instead.
- **Thesis use:** action-failure scenario
- **Topics:** action replacement; execution failure; severity
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
The Probabilistic Action Robust MDP models imperfect control by mixing the agent's action with an adversarial action. The parameter α determines how often control is effectively transferred away from the agent, providing an explicit severity parameter for action-execution uncertainty.

### Limitation
A stochastic no-op or random replacement in a GridWorld is less adversarial than the paper's worst-case replacement and must be labeled as a different perturbation model.

## Evidence E2 — Abrupt action replacement and continuous action perturbation are different uncertainty models
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Sections 1, 3 and 4
- **Claim:** PR-MDP and NR-MDP formalize distinct types of action uncertainty.
- **Thesis use:** perturbation taxonomy
- **Topics:** PR-MDP; NR-MDP; action noise
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
PR-MDP represents sporadic loss of control in which another action is executed, while NR-MDP represents a perturbation added to a continuous action. The authors connect the former to abrupt disturbances and the latter to persistent forces or parameter mismatch. These mechanisms should not be merged under a single unspecified label such as “action noise.”

## Evidence E3 — Robustness strength creates a task-dependent performance trade-off
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** experimental sections on α sensitivity and robustness evaluation
- **Claim:** The amount of adversarial action influence materially affects performance, and a stronger perturbation is not always better for training.
- **Thesis use:** severity ablation
- **Topics:** α; robustness-performance trade-off; nominal cost
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
The experiments show that the useful range of α depends on the task and robustness formulation. Too little adversarial influence may provide insufficient protection, whereas too much can make optimization difficult or prevent the agent from learning a good nominal policy. Severity should therefore be varied and reported rather than tuned implicitly on final test conditions.

## Evidence E4 — Deep adversarial training does not inherit tabular guarantees automatically
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** deep-RL extension and discussion
- **Claim:** The theoretical policy-iteration results apply to the idealized robust formulation, while the neural actor/adversary implementation introduces approximation and optimization effects not covered by the same guarantees.
- **Thesis use:** theory/practice boundary
- **Topics:** deep RL; convergence; function approximation
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
The deep implementation trains nonlinear function approximators with gradient-based updates. Empirical behavior can therefore differ from the idealized max-min policy-iteration analysis. Theoretical properties of the tabular or exact formulation should not be presented as convergence guarantees for an arbitrary adversarially trained neural agent.

## Evidence E5 — Action robustness is static robustness, not post-change recovery
- **Type:** thesis-safe inference from the formulation
- **Location:** Sections 1–6
- **Claim:** The method optimizes a policy against a predefined action-uncertainty criterion and does not detect a changepoint or relearn after an unannounced regime switch.
- **Thesis use:** robustness/resilience boundary
- **Topics:** robust policy; change detection; recovery
- **Status:** verified

## Avoid overclaiming
Use this source to formalize action-execution uncertainty and robustness trade-offs. Do not treat PR-MDP/NR-MDP robustness as evidence of online resilience or assume that adversarial replacement is equivalent to ordinary stochastic actuation failure.
