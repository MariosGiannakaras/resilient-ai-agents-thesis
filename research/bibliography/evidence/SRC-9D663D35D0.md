# Evidence — Robust Markov Decision Processes without Model Estimation

## E1 — Many robust-MDP methods remain model-based in memory and computation
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Abstract; Section 1
- **Claim:** Prior robust-MDP approaches often estimate or store a full transition model and may also assume an exact oracle for the inner distributionally robust optimization problem.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
Yang et al. identify two practical barriers in robust-MDP learning. First, explicit transition estimation can require storage on the order of `|S|^2|A|`. Second, sample-complexity analyses can hide the cost of repeatedly solving the inner DRO problem by assuming access to a strong exact oracle that may not exist in practice.

### Thesis use
Do not label a robust method “model-free” solely because the environment model is unknown initially; report whether a transition tensor or exact robust-optimization oracle is required during learning.

### Citation
Yang et al. (2023), Abstract and Section 1.

## E2 — Penalized robust optimization trades an explicit uncertainty constraint for a penalty term
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Section 1; Section 2
- **Claim:** The paper replaces the constrained DRO form with a penalized formulation connected through Lagrangian duality so stochastic-gradient methods can be used without the same oracle assumption.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
The authors introduce a surrogate robust-MDP formulation in which deviation from the nominal transition distribution is penalized in the objective rather than enforced through an explicit divergence-ball constraint. The dual representation has bounded-gradient properties that permit sample-based stochastic optimization and enables a Q-learning-type solution without repeatedly calling an exact inner DRO solver.

### Context and limits
The constrained and penalized formulations have different robustness parameters and are related mathematically rather than being literally identical optimization problems.

### Thesis use
If a penalized robust baseline is considered, report the penalty parameter and distinguish it from an uncertainty-set radius.

### Citation
Yang et al. (2023), Sections 1–2.

## E3 — Robust Q-learning can reduce storage to `O(|S||A|)`
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Abstract; Sections 4–5
- **Claim:** The proposed Q-learning-type algorithms avoid storing the full transition model and use action-value storage on the order of the state–action table.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
Using the penalized dual formulation, the paper derives robust Q-learning-style algorithms whose storage requirement is `O(|S||A|)` rather than the model-based transition-table requirement. Finite-sample analyses are provided under both a generative-model setting and a Markovian single-trajectory setting.

### Context and limits
Lower storage does not imply ordinary tabular-Q complexity: the robust updates introduce additional stochastic optimization and robustness parameters.

### Thesis use
Use this source only to justify feasibility of a resource-aware robust tabular comparator, with measured update-time overhead and memory reported explicitly.

### Citation
Yang et al. (2023), Abstract and Sections 4–5.

## E4 — Generative-model access and one-trajectory online data are different information regimes
- **Type:** faithful paraphrase
- **Location:** Sections 4–5
- **Claim:** The paper separately analyzes independent sampling through a generative model and Markovian data obtained from one trajectory under a behavior policy.
- **Status:** verified

### Faithful paraphrase
In the generative-model regime, samples can be requested for particular state–action pairs. In the Markovian regime, the learner receives only the transition generated at the state–action pair actually visited along a trajectory. The latter creates correlated data and dependence on the behavior policy's coverage and mixing properties.

### Thesis use
Record simulator query access separately from ordinary environment interactions; do not give one method a generative oracle and call the interaction budgets matched.

### Citation
Yang et al. (2023), Sections 4–5.

## E5 — Robust-MDP optimization addresses in-set worst-case model uncertainty, not online changepoint recovery
- **Type:** scope inference grounded in the formulation
- **Location:** Sections 1–2
- **Claim:** The robust objective optimizes against transition distributions in a predefined uncertainty region around a nominal model; it does not define temporal shift detection, context memory, or detector-triggered relearning.
- **Status:** verified

### Thesis use
Keep `robust_model_uncertainty` separate from `online_resilience_after_unknown_shift` and include out-of-set structural changes as a separate stress test.

### Citation
Yang et al. (2023), Sections 1–2.

## E6 — Resource-aware comparison needs more than return
- **Type:** thesis-protocol synthesis
- **Location:** Motivation and algorithmic contributions
- **Claim:** The paper explicitly motivates its method through memory, oracle, and sample-access costs in addition to robust performance.
- **Status:** verified

### Thesis use
For a robust Q-learning pilot, report clean return, in-set disturbed return, out-of-set return, conservativeness gap, interaction count, oracle/generative queries, memory footprint, and update-time cost.

### Citation
Yang et al. (2023), Abstract and Section 1.
