# Research notes and author fragments

This directory accepts useful writing material that is not necessarily a bibliographic source.

Examples include:

- author-written definitions or draft paragraphs;
- synthesis notes assembled from several sources;
- observations, hypotheses, reminders, and terminology notes;
- partial text whose title, author, URL, or citation identity is unknown.

Files here are exported to `research-corpus/notes/` exactly as stored. They do not require citation metadata and must not be deleted merely because they are not citation-ready. When a note supports a formal thesis claim, the final thesis text should still connect that claim to verified evidence where appropriate.
