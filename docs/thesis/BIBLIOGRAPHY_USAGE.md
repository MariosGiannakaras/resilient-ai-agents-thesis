# Thesis Bibliography Usage

Final Greek thesis claims use only identifiers present in `research/bibliography/citation-ready/manifest.csv` and the corresponding verified evidence and analysis at the pinned source commit.

Full-corpus sources, rejected/theory-only records, `MAT-*` materials, aggregates, and notes may guide research, terminology, drafting, and gap discovery but are not formal citations. Translation into Greek occurs during writing and never replaces the original-language evidence record.

When non-citation material is required for a final claim, record an exact promotion task in `ThesisBibliography`, complete its verification upstream, and synchronize a new immutable export before citing it.
