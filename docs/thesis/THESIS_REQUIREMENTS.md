# Thesis Requirements

## Confirmed format and language

- Main thesis language: Greek.
- Final deliverable: Microsoft Word (`.docx`).
- Official Greek and English titles must match the approved application until formally changed.
- Current Department guidance and supervisor instructions prevail over historical examples.
- Final conclusions must be based on real frozen results.
- Detailed future tool/ownership/manual-asset workflow is `docs/thesis/WP7_WP8_TOOL_WORKFLOW.md`; it is planning only until the explicit WP7 approval gate passes.

## Verified Department-guideline snapshot

The official Department writing-guidelines PDF reviewed during bootstrap specifies, unless superseded:

### Required front/main/end structure

1. Title page in thesis language.
2. Title page in the other language.
3. Copyright and plagiarism declarations.
4. Optional dedication.
5. Optional acknowledgements.
6. Greek summary and keywords.
7. English abstract and keywords.
8. Automatic table of contents.
9. Lists of figures and tables.
10. Alphabetical index/glossary of terms.
11. Main numbered chapters.
12. Bibliography/references/web sources.
13. Appendices where needed.

### Indicative scale

- No strict page minimum/maximum is stated.
- The reviewed guidance gives an indicative range of approximately 70–140 pages and 20,000–40,000 words, excluding bibliography and appendices.
- Greek and English summaries are indicated at approximately 250–300 words, commonly with about five keywords each.

These remain guidance, not a substitute for current supervisor/template confirmation at T-700.

### Methodology and technical content

The thesis must document:

- research problem, purpose and questions;
- methodological choices and rejected alternatives;
- experiment conditions, parameters and quantitative indicators;
- data/evidence sources;
- tools, equipment and software;
- enough detail for repetition and verification;
- technical analysis/design/implementation;
- results organized by experiments/conditions;
- comparison with relevant literature;
- limitations/threats to validity and future work.

### Formatting snapshot

- A4 page.
- Times New Roman, 11 pt main text.
- 1.5 line spacing.
- 14 pt headings and 12 pt subheadings, preferably bold.
- Numbered heading styles suitable for automatic TOC.
- New chapters start on a new page.
- Tables use readable fonts, not below 10 pt according to the reviewed guidance.
- Figures/tables need numbering, captions and source attribution when not original.
- Equations use equation tools and numbering where appropriate.
- Technical terms should be introduced consistently, with Greek/English form on first use.
- One citation style must be applied consistently; the reviewed guideline lists APA 6, Chicago 16, IEEE 2006 or Harvard Anglia 2008.

The final citation style remains open and must be resolved from current supervisor/Department/template guidance at T-700 rather than guessed now.

## Writing and evidence requirements

- Final thesis drafting begins only after the explicit pre-WP7 approval gate.
- Results/conclusions cannot be written as final before frozen accepted evidence exists.
- Every quantitative thesis claim maps to frozen result/figure/table IDs.
- Every external factual claim needing support cites verified citation-ready evidence.
- No invented authors, titles, DOI, venues, quotations, page numbers, run values or statistical results.
- Negative, null and unexpected findings are reported rather than hidden.
- Limitations, threats to validity and protocol deviations are explicit.
- Terminology remains consistent between Greek thesis, English abstract, code, protocol and application.
- Historical v1.0/R0 evidence is labelled historical where discussed; it is not silently mixed into final-v1.1 primary evidence.
- Final F0/C0/D0 names, frozen settings, conditions, repetitions and statistical roles come from the accepted final protocol/evidence package.

## Microsoft Word implementation requirements

The review-ready/final thesis should use Word's document model rather than manual numbering wherever practical:

- real Heading styles for chapter/subchapter hierarchy;
- automatic Table of Contents generated from headings;
- Word caption fields for figures/tables/equations;
- automatic list/table of figures and tables generated from captions;
- Word cross-references to headings/figures/tables/equations instead of manually typed numbers/page references;
- equation tooling and numbering where required;
- consistent document styles;
- controlled section/page breaks and pagination;
- final update of fields/captions/TOC/lists before freeze.

Manual formatting must not break automatic numbering/cross-references or create a second numbering system.

## Figures, tables, screenshots and animated application media

- Scientific result figures/tables are generated from versioned frozen data/scripts.
- Application screenshots illustrate implementation/workflow/state; they do not replace statistical figures for quantitative claims.
- User-captured screenshots/GIF/video must follow the `ASSET-*` placement/provenance contract in `WP7_WP8_TOOL_WORKFLOW.md`.
- Every requested manual capture receives exact page/state/run/config/crop/content instructions and exact thesis/slide placement/caption guidance.
- The core thesis argument must remain understandable in static print/PDF form. Any useful animation therefore has a static screenshot/figure fallback.
- Do not make an essential scientific claim depend on GIF/video playback.
- Every original generated figure or user capture records the relevant run/config/artifact/protocol identity when applicable.

## Automation requirements

The project should generate or validate:

- figures and tables from versioned data/scripts;
- figure/table/equation caption and cross-reference registers where useful;
- bibliography records from verified metadata;
- glossary/acronym list;
- claim-to-source/result/artifact register;
- manual application-asset placement register;
- Word formatting/field-update checklist;
- final link/cross-reference/field checks;
- thesis-to-defense evidence mapping.

Automation must not generate unsupported scientific interpretation.

## Required final quality checks

- Official title/Department/person metadata.
- Current template/submission guidance.
- Heading hierarchy and automatic TOC.
- Captions, numbering and cross-references.
- Lists of figures/tables.
- Greek/English summaries and keywords.
- Citation-reference consistency.
- Figure/table legibility and source/evidence attribution.
- Application screenshot/media provenance and static fallbacks.
- Equation formatting.
- Terminology/glossary consistency.
- Spelling/grammar and academic register.
- All result claims traceable to repository evidence.
- No provisional/tuning/debug evidence presented as final.
- No private material unintentionally included in public/presentation outputs.
- Final `.docx` visually inspected in Microsoft Word after all fields are updated.
