**Scope:** metadata-level review of recent records associated with the Department of Informatics and Computer Engineering, University of West Attica.

**Authority:** supporting evidence only. The current official Department guidance and the supervisor's instructions always prevail.

## Purpose

Recent approved theses were checked to understand the range of accepted topics and document structures near the project domain. This review does **not** establish formatting rules and does not replace inspection of the full documents before final writing.

## Conclusions that may safely be used

- The Department repository contains both Greek- and English-titled works and topics combining theory, implementation and evaluation.
- A software artifact may be presented as part of a thesis when it supports a documented research or engineering objective.
- Comparative evaluation is an established academic structure, but the exact models, metrics and experimental protocol for this thesis must come from its own topic and literature.

## What may not be inferred

- No recent thesis is an official Word template.
- Page layout, citation style or chapter ordering must not be copied merely because a thesis was approved.
- Metadata does not prove the quality or correctness of a document's methodology.
- Master's theses must not be used to redefine this project's academic classification.

## Required follow-up before final writing

1. Recheck the Department's official writing guidance for a newer revision.
2. Locate the current official Word template and submission package, if available.
3. Inspect a small set of full, recent undergraduate theses from the same Department for practical conventions only.
4. Record any supervisor-specific requirements in `docs/context/CONFIRMED_REQUIREMENTS.md` and `docs/decisions/DECISION_LOG.md`.
