# Thesis Source Material

Place authoritative primary thesis material here, including:
- approved application or official description,
- supervisor-provided brief or instructions,
- official forms/templates,
- signed or scanned primary documents.

Accepted formats may include PDF, DOCX, images or plain text, subject to repository-size and privacy review.

## Preservation rules

- Do not modify the original file.
- Record filename, origin, date, checksum and privacy status in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.md`.
- Create a separate processed/transcribed/translated derivative when needed.
- Do not overwrite the original with OCR or cleanup.
- Codex must inspect this material before freezing the research plan.
- Private/personal material must not be included in public releases without redaction.

The official application has been verified locally and approved for unchanged storage at `ThesisApplication.pdf`. Repository presence must be verified before the bootstrap is marked complete.
